Mean stage index

Stage composition

100% stacked bars, strains side by side, one panel per dose × timepoint. Numbers inside segments are % of animals detected; n above each bar is how many that was — watch it move.

Body-size distribution

Every detected animal, no stage bins — the continuous quantity the classifier is discretising. Kernel density in log space (growth is multiplicative), curves scaled to equal height, ▲ marks the median.

Quality control

Whether there were enough animals to measure, and whether the four quadrants of a plate agree. % of control compares each condition with the lowest dose of the same strain at the same timepoint — its own control, never another strain's and never another day's.
